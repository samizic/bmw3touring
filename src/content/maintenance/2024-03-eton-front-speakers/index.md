The 330d has the **Harman Kardon** surround system: 9 channels and 16 speakers. It sounds good,
but the front speakers are the weak spot: the tweeters are a little harsh, and the mid-bass lacks
punch. **ETON**, a German car-audio maker, builds drop-in replacements for exactly these BMW
speaker positions.

## What was replaced

All three front positions, mid and tweeter in each:

- **Left and right doors:** ETON **UG BMW B100 W** kit, a 10 cm mid plus a silk-dome tweeter per side
- **Centre of the dashboard:** ETON **B100 XC N**, a coaxial speaker with the tweeter built into the
  middle of the mid

The rear speakers and the under-seat subwoofers stay original.

Everything is **plug and play**: the ETON speakers use the factory connectors and mounting points.
No cutting, no soldering, no extra wiring and no coding.

## Tools

- **T20 Torx** screwdriver
- A set of **plastic trim levers**, for the door trim and the centre speaker cover

## Time

About **10 minutes** for the centre speaker and about **15 minutes per door**, so well under an hour
for the whole front stage.

## Centre speaker

1. Lift the speaker grille on top of the dashboard with a plastic lever, starting at an edge.
2. Unscrew the original mid (T20) and unplug it.
3. Plug in the ETON B100 XC N, drop it into the same position and screw it down.
4. Clip the grille back on.

The original centre tweeter can stay where it is. It won't play any more, because the ETON's
built-in coaxial tweeter takes over.

## Doors

1. Remove the door trim panel. Undo its two T20 screws: one behind the small cover next to the
   door handle (pry the cover off with a trim lever), and one inside the door pull. Then work
   around the edge with plastic levers to release the clips, lift the panel off and unplug the
   switches and lights.
2. Undo the three T20 screws holding the original mid, unplug it and fit the ETON B100 W in its
   place with the same screws.
3. Swap the original tweeter for the ETON tweeter. It plugs into the same connector.
4. Reconnect the plugs and clip the trim panel back on.

## Result

The sound is **clearer and more natural**, mainly thanks to ETON's silk-dome tweeters, which are
smoother than the originals. **Mid-bass is more precise and has more kick.** The difference is
obvious straight away, and the tone settings in iDrive are the same as before the upgrade.
