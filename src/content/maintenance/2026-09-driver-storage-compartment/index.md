Early G20/G21 cars had a small folding compartment in the dash, low down to the left of the
steering column and below the light switch. It's handy for sunglasses, a card or a parking disc. BMW
deleted it on later production cars, but the dash moulding still carries its outline. Cut along
that outline and the original part fits straight in.

## Parts

The parts are listed on the right. The compartment comes in different colours and versions, so
check the part number against your interior in the parts catalogue before ordering. Used
compartments come up regularly on eBay.

## Tools

- Printed template (download on the right), masking tape and a marker
- Rotary tool (Dremel) with a cutting disc, or a sharp utility knife
- Sandpaper or a file for the edges
- Screwdriver for the mounting screws
- Vacuum cleaner, plus plenty of cloths or plastic sheet to cover the cabin

## How it's done

1. **Mask the cabin.** Cutting the dash makes a lot of fine dust. Cover the seat, steering wheel,
   centre console and footwell.
2. **Fit the template.** Print the PDF at 100 % (no scaling) and check it against the compartment
   itself before cutting anything. Tape it over the moulded outline below the light switch.
3. **Score the skin first.** Cutting the vinyl skin along the line with a knife, then pulling out
   the foam behind it, keeps the dust down when the rotary tool goes through the hard plastic.
4. **Cut inside the line.** Start small and work outwards. There is fibre insulation and wiring
   behind the panel, so keep the cuts shallow.
5. **Trim to fit.** File and sand the opening until the compartment slides in snugly. Colour any
   light-coloured cut edges with a black marker so they don't show through the gaps.
6. **Clean out** the insulation fluff and dust before fitting.
7. **Fit the compartment.** Screw the internal screw to the metal frame first. Only drill the last
   two holes at the top once the box is in place and correctly aligned, as the template's
   author advises.
